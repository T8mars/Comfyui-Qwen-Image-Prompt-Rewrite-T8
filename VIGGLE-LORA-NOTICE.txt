This release redistributes a modified version of the LoRA adapter from Viggle/Qwen-Image-2.1-viggle-turbo:
https://huggingface.co/Viggle/Qwen-Image-2.1-viggle-turbo

Viggle trained and released the 4-step distilled Qwen-Image-2.1 LoRA. T8mars converted its Diffusers tensor keys to native ComfyUI Qwen Image 2.1 model paths and renamed the output file. The BF16 tensor payload was copied byte for byte; no additional training or weight changes were made. The converted LoRA is separate from Viggle's full fine-tuned transformer.
